What is the

Smart Home

Smart house involves the control and automation of lighting, heating, ventilation, air conditioning and security, as well as home appliances such as washer/dryers, ovens or refrigerators/freezers.

Wi-Fi is often used for remote monitoring and control.

Modern systems consist of switches and sensors connected to a central hub from which the system is controlled with a mobile phone, tablet computer or a web interface.
